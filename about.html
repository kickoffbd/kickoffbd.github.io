About KickOffBD

KickOffBD is a free football hub for fans in Bangladesh and everywhere else. It brings together match scores, league tables, top scorers, highlight links and information about where each competition can be watched, all on one simple page that works on any phone.

What you will find here

  • Scores and fixtures for matches from yesterday to the day after tomorrow, with a filter for each league.
  • League tables and top scorers for the major European leagues, including matches played, goals and assists.
  • Where to watch, a guide to licensed platforms that show each competition in Bangladesh.
  • Highlights, links to the official YouTube channels of the leagues.

What we do not do

KickOffBD does not broadcast, host or link to unauthorised live streams. Football broadcasting rights are expensive and protected, so we only point fans to official and licensed sources. Highlight links open on the publisher’s own channel.

Where the data comes from

Scores, tables, scorers and club logos are provided by football-data.org. On the free plan, scores can be delayed by a short time, so please do not treat KickOffBD as a source for betting or time-critical decisions. Club names, logos and flags belong to their respective owners, and we use them only to identify teams.

Independence

KickOffBD is an independent website. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any league, club, federation or broadcaster mentioned on the site.
