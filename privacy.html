Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you visit kickoffbd.github.io (“KickOffBD”, “we”, “our”). We keep things simple: you do not need an account, and we do not ask you for personal details.

Information we collect

KickOffBD has no sign-up, comment or contact form, and we do not collect your name, email address or phone number through the site. If you email us, we receive your email address and message, and we use them only to reply.

Information processed automatically

Like every website, loading our pages involves some technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the time of your visit. This is handled by the services that make the site work:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may keep server logs under GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Cloudflare runs a small relay (a Worker) that fetches football data for us. It processes your IP address while delivering that data, under Cloudflare’s privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts supplies our typeface, so your browser contacts Google to load it. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • football-data.org serves club logos and flags, which your browser downloads from its servers.

Cookies and local storage

We do not set tracking cookies. To keep the site fast and to stay within data limits, your browser stores a temporary copy of football data (scores, tables, scorers) in its local storage for a few minutes. It contains no personal information, and you can clear it any time in your browser settings.

Analytics

We do not currently use analytics tools. If we add one, we will update this page and name the provider.

Advertising

KickOffBD may show advertising, for example through Google AdSense, in the future. Advertising partners such as Google may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on your earlier visits to this and other sites. You can manage personalised ads in Google Ads Settings or learn more at aboutads.info. Where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent first. We will update this policy before any ads appear.

Links to other sites

Our pages link to services such as YouTube and to broadcaster websites. Those sites have their own privacy practices, and we are not responsible for them.

Children

KickOffBD is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to delete or export. If you believe we hold information about you, contact us and we will respond promptly. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under local privacy laws, and you can also contact the services listed above directly.

Accuracy of information

Football data on KickOffBD can be delayed or incomplete. We try to be accurate, but we make no guarantee, and the site should not be used for betting or other time-critical decisions.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
